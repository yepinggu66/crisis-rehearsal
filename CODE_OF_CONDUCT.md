# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Crisis Rehearsal to be a project where anyone can contribute — especially people who
care about honest measurement, reproducibility, and saying uncomfortable things about what did
not work. We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This applies to maintainers, contributors, and anyone representing the project.

## Our standards

**Expected:**

- Be direct about the work and generous with the person. Critique the number, not the author.
- Back claims with evidence — a data file, a citation, a reproduction command.
- Be honest about uncertainty and mistakes. This project publishes its failures; doing the same
  in a PR or an issue is the norm, not a weakness.
- Respect that this project touches real events involving real people. Do not add content that
  attacks or speculates about private individuals, and keep personal data out.
- Accept a "no" with an explanation, and give one when you say no.

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory or sexualised comments, imagery, or attention.
- Doxxing: publishing someone's private information, or adding private individuals' data to the
  event corpus.
- Deliberately misrepresenting data, sources, results, or someone else's work.
- Pressuring maintainers to certify, endorse or overstate the project's capabilities.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a problem in good faith.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that are not aligned
with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate the reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, chats)
and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to **yepinggu@outlook.com** with
the subject `[CONDUCT]`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Security vulnerabilities are handled separately under [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) — do not
report them to a public issue.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these steps, scaled to the severity and pattern of behaviour:

1. **Correction** — a private message explaining the problem and why it was inappropriate; a
   public apology may be requested. Suitable for a single, unclear incident.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with consequences for continued behaviour; no interaction
   with the people involved for a period. Suitable for a single serious incident or a pattern.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from interaction or contribution. Suitable for a serious
   violation or sustained inappropriate behaviour.
4. **Permanent ban** — permanent removal from project spaces. Suitable for a pattern of
   violations, harassment, or aggression, or for doxxing.

Maintainers may skip steps for clear-cut serious violations. A maintainer who is the subject of
a report will not participate in that report's handling.
