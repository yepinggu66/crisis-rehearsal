## 1. Logline

As two visionary engineers propel humanity into the spacefaring and AI-computing era—one carrying the fire of civilization while enduring relentless backlash after open-sourcing breakthrough technology, the other caught inside a $3-trillion boardroom storm atop the world's most critical silicon empire—their parallel timelines collide over the future of intelligence, exposing the crushing human cost of building the future.

---

## 2. Why This Storyline Works (Not Just Entertainment — A Global Tech Explainer)

Most audiences consume headlines about rockets, AI chips, and trillion-dollar stock swings without ever grasping the **physical, engineering, and governance pressure** endured by the builders behind them.

*PROMETHEUS & SILICON* weaves two real-world-inspired, high-voltage business thrillers into a single narrative:

### A-Plot: The Promethean Fire (Elon Musk-Inspired Arc)
- **The Mission**: First-principles engineering at the edge of bankruptcy—reusable orbital rockets, energy transition, and pushing human civilization beyond a single planet.
- **The Paradox of Open Source**: Choosing to open-source core patents and technical blueprints to accelerate humanity, only to discover the coldest law of modern business: **goodwill openness is rewarded not with gratitude, but with coordinated smear campaigns, short-seller attacks, and rivals weaponizing the very fire you shared**.
- **Embedded 30-Second Explainers**: *Why first-principles physics breaks traditional aerospace budgets* and *how open-source ecosystems can be turned against their own creators*.

### B-Plot: The $3-Trillion Silicon Storm (Jensen Huang / 2026 $NVDA Exit-Inspired Arc)
- **The Structural Tension**: When a semiconductor company grows larger than sovereign GDP and becomes the choke point of global AI, founder-led flat governance collides head-on with Wall Street risk committees, regulatory oversight, and institutional board politics.
- **The Rashomon 72-Hour Storm**: Told across three interlocking perspectives—the board's fiduciary fear, Wall Street's ruthless repricing, and the founder's sudden isolation from the 18-year software-and-silicon empire he built from scratch.
- **Embedded 30-Second Explainers**: *Why one software moat (CUDA) + one packaging bottleneck (CoWoS/HBM) controls global AI* and *what "Key-Person Risk" actually means on Wall Street*.

## 3. Three-Act Narrative Arc

### Act I — Lighting the Fire (0–35 min)
- **Cold Open (Late Autumn 2026, 3:00 AM Silicon Valley)**: Intercut two suffocating moments—a rocket pad counting down to ignition amid a storm of hostile headlines, and a glass boardroom in Santa Clara where a sealed independent investigation dossier slides across a conference table.
- **Parallel Origins**: Both engineers bet everything against Wall Street consensus—one on reusable rockets and open technology, the other on programmable GPUs years before deep learning existed.

### Act II — Backlash, Open-Source Betrayal & The 72-Hour Coup (35–105 min)
- **A-Plot Escalation**: After open-sourcing key technologies, the builder faces coordinated media distortion, regulatory crossfire, and relentless public vilification while sleeping on the factory floor to solve real physics bottlenecks.
- **B-Plot Escalation**: At the absolute peak of a $3-trillion valuation, a 72-hour governance earthquake erupts. No cartoon villains—only the terrifying momentum of institutional risk aversion, short-seller opportunism, and boardroom power dynamics.
- **The Midpoint Collision**: Both timelines intersect over the global race for hyperscale AI superclusters—confronting the same brutal truth: *once your creation becomes civilizational infrastructure, you no longer own your destiny.*

### Act III — Aftershock & The Verdict of History (105–140 min)
- **Climax (Hans Zimmer-Scale Score)**: A-Plot answers months of smears not with PR spin, but with an undeniable physical engineering triumph caught live on camera. Meanwhile, in B-Plot's aftermath, rumours of a $50B rival semiconductor venture send shockwaves across Wall Street and hyperscaler boardrooms.
- **Final Question**: Pulling back from orbital rocket trails to glowing global data centers, the film leaves audiences with one haunting question: *How does humanity treat the relentless builders who carry its future on their shoulders?*
